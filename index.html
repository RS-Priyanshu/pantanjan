Welcome to a Living Testament of Love

Darbar-e-Panjatan Paak Trust is the heart of Hazaribagh's spiritual and social conscience — a living testament to the eternal love for Muhammad ﷺ and his Holy Household (Ahle Bait). We are a community of hearts united in devotion, translating the supreme values of the Panjatan Paak into tangible acts of compassion, justice, and enlightenment.

Here, faith is alive. It feeds the hungry, heals the sick, educates the curious, and comforts the weary. Every action is an offering (Nazrana) at the sacred threshold of those who taught humanity how to live with purpose, dignity, and unconditional love.

True Ishq-e-Ahle Bait is proven not only in mourning but in building; not only in remembrance but in action. Darbar-e-Panjatan Paak is where spiritual reverence meets social revolution.

Our spiritual lineage: Connected to the ethos of Sayyed Haji Waris Ali Shah (R.A.) of the Warsiyya tradition, we propagate the message of love for Ahl-e-Bait through service to all of humanity. To join this Darbar is to become part of this sacred spiritual connection (Nisbat).

BRIEF BIOGRAPHY OF 12 IMAMS

The Imams are twelve members of the Ahl al-Bayt of the Prophet (PBUH&HF) who are the successors of the Prophet and after him the Imam of the Islamic community. The first Imam is Imam Ali (AS) and the other Imams are his children and grandchildren and Sayyida Fatimah (SA).

They have been appointed to the Imamate by Allah (SWT) and have characteristics such as infallibility, superiority, knowledge of the unseen and the right of intercession. Imams (AS) have all the duties of the Prophet (PBUH) except receiving revelation and bringing Sharia.

Eleven Imams were martyred, and the last of them, the Promised Mahdi (AJTF), is absent. He will reappear in the future and fill the earth with justice.

✨ Vision & Mission

To cultivate a community that mirrors the noble character (Akhlaq) of the Panjatan Paak — where every individual lives with faith, acts with justice, serves with compassion, and leads with wisdom.

Our Mission:

  • Ignite with sincere love (Muhabbat)
  • Embody teachings: justice of Ali, patience of Fatima, peace of Hasan, sacrifice of Hussain
  • Serve unconditionally (Khidmat-e-Khalq)
  • Bridge divides through interfaith harmony
  • Empower generations with spiritual clarity

🔍 Governance & Transparency

Operating with Sacred Trust (Amanah)

We operate as a registered trust under a structured, ethical framework. Our governance is built on accountability and the sacred responsibility we hold towards our community and supporters.

  • Structured governance, clear roles
